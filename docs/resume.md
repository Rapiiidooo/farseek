# Farseek handoff

Farseek is an original browser adventure starring Nyra Varen, built around traversal, a held relay orb and doors between worlds. The local repository is `farseek/`; progress retains the `far-door-` storage keys. Keep names, logos and signature designs of existing franchises out of the game; see [the style lock](style-lock.md).

## Traps met on the way

- Toggle running before testing off-edge falls; walking deliberately stays on the lip (`Hero.ground`).
- Schedule Web Audio starts and ramps at or after `ctx.currentTime`; a fresh or offline context starts at 0 and a negative time throws (`hiss` in `voice.js`).
- Tie a scene's voice to its frames; Web Audio keeps playing under the pause menu, so scenes call `voice.alive()` and silent frames pause their lines (`watchdog`).
- Skip story scenes in route scripts before scripted presses; a cinematic swallows E, R and movement, so a press landing as a scene starts is lost (`sceneSkipped`).
- Check a delayed trigger's condition before its timer; with a zero delay `held < after` never returns and the ice scene fired on its first frame (`Scene.update`).
- Import `object-copy.js` before cloning assets whose `userData` declares parts; `Object3D.copy` otherwise serializes each part with its geometry on every clone (`holdsResource`).
- Isolate warm-up draws with layers, never visibility; hiding a parent hides its lights and changes every program key (`warmBatches`).
- Request `autoUpdate = false` shadow maps before any offscreen draw; an unrendered map fails every lit draw with a sampler mismatch (`statics`).
- Draw clipped portal variants during preparation; a program's first draw blocks for its link even after `compileAsync` (`onFirstUse`).
- Move the explorer with a moving support through `hero.carry`; adding to `hero.pos` leaves planted feet in the world and stretches the legs (`carried`).
- Clamp every custom-shader `pow` base at zero; a unit dot product exceeds 1 by rounding, and bloom spreads one NaN texel into a half-black frame (`LuminosityHighPassShader`).
- Fade transparent sheets the follow camera can enter by their distance to the lens; the near plane cuts them into screen-sized wedges (`vLens`).
- Keep the follow camera's turn small while hanging, climbing, holding a block or on a climbing wall; those states read input relative to the camera (`reframe`). A swing reads its stick in the rope's own frame instead, so the camera may turn to watch the arc (`swingStep`).
- Test Nyra's body spheres against the box between the lens and the near plane, and draw that plane in rather than out near her; one-by-one frustum side planes and a near plane pushed to 0.38 m hid her with the lens half a metre off her shoulder (`clips`, `NEARS`).
- Complete a tutorial step on its outcome, not on the action: the court block counted as moved while it still sat in the beam, leaving the objective on a dark mirror (`segments.some`).
- Read controls through `input.binding(action)` and `input.label(action)`, never fixed codes; prompts, cards and menus follow remapped keys only through them (`binding`).
- Test a swimmer against the whole door opening; a walking-height window lets a high swimmer pass through without crossing (`returnCrossed`).
- Keep a panel hole off its outline's foot; three r186 fills a hole that touches the outline, as it hid every stela glyph (`glyph_stela`).
- Give cloth and rope a redder hue than pale khaki; low-saturation fabric turns mint in the court's cool shade (`expedition_tent`).
- Read surfaces over the bog with `floorAt`; `terrainAt` returns the basin floor (`forest-bog.js`).
- Plan forest paths through `world.planPath`; `WardenPath` sees only `world.boxes`, not the measured rocks and trees (`forest-paths.js`).
- Solve the two deepest contacts together; pushing out of one at a time shuttles a body between faces in a corner (`meet`).
- Derive reflection normals from fitted geometry; asset merging bakes mesh rotations into vertices (`CourtMirrors`).
- Keep masonry cores at `minY` and close exposed undersides; missing faces reveal the clear color beneath platforms (`FaceBuilder`).
