# Follow-ups of the owner review and the epics round

The owner review and the nine epics that followed it are committed on `main` and public since release `20261010-74fd2cec631f` ([epics round receipt](../receipts/verification-epics-round/README.md), [release receipt](../receipts/verification-seven-level-release/README.md)). These items remain.

- **Evidence in Git:** the rounds' receipts (about 13.7 GB in 23,000 files, mostly PNG frames) and `media/` (about 700 MB of films) stay untracked. Decide what Git keeps (READMEs and JSON, for instance) and where films live (release assets, as for the jam trailer).
- **Owner hand test:** replay the seven worlds on the owner's machine: the isles' rope swings and draped ropes, the ice axes and the castle ivy, the sea's currents and flooded stair, the frost lynx, the orb's pull and shield, difficulty and health refill, Mira's scenes and voice, the vault's six trials and the reveal, and the credits after the vault.
- **Physical devices and listening:** a phone in portrait and landscape (touch layout, the phone residency path of `worlds/manager.js` and `worlds/gpu-budget.js`, the court without its composer), a gamepad, slower desktop graphics, Safari's decoding of the voice clips, and the mix, Mira's voice and Nyra's efforts on headphones. Measured loudness does not establish any of these.
- **Frozen falls:** the scarred falls read as a flat striped slab at a distance (`Curtain` in `frost-climbs.js`); sculpting them needs the climb's hand and pick contacts checked again (`scripts/animation-check.mjs`, `receipts/verification-traversal-walls/walls-review.mjs`).
- **Built in code:** the castle's outer wall and bastion, the isle swing stakes (sunk a few centimetres into a dome) and the unknown world behind the vault's next door (a scene of boxes) want a model round through the recipe workflow.
- **Colour only cues:** the castle keeper's counter window and the reticle still change colour alone (`hazard-marks.js` gives ground warnings shapes).
- **Restored worlds:** on desktops a world brought back after its targets were released reruns its whole preparation during play, and the cold back crossing from the forest to the frozen reach keeps one unexplained 83 ms frame; pipelines for a quality chosen mid-game build at their first draw (`tasks/graphics-startup-budget.md`).
- **Start window:** a start from the menus now waits only for the worlds around it (`prepare`), and the others prepare behind play, held back 2.5 s; starts by address still settle every world. Served locally, play begins about 1 s after New game (4.7 s before), 3.4 s after Continue at the sea (6.6 s) and 3.5 s at the vault (4.8 s); under 300 ms of emulated latency at 1 MB/s it begins 6.1 s after the click instead of 31.2 s, and the recipe's phone and desktop live gates pass locally. A title preparation step already running at the click can still overrun the first frame of play once, by about 85 ms under the loading screen's fade. On the public release `20261012-ef085952c3c5` play begins 2.3 to 2.8 s after New game and the desktop live gate passes; the phone gate still fails because it holds its control a fixed 1.5 s after its tap, while the phone emulation is still under the loading screen (`receipts/verification-mute-start-menu-release/`).
- **Weight:** the strict desktop Fast gate measures 15.6 MB against its 10 MB budget (the title now prepares all seven worlds; this round added about 2.7 MB of audio and 1.5 MB of code). Candidates: binary explorer data instead of base64 modules, compressed audio sprites, loading farther worlds after the first door.
- **Scene timing:** a player who reaches the checkpoint booth within 5.6 s of arriving never hears Mira's radio (`radioScene` in `mira-scenes.js`); a player who never pumps the relay swing can fall short of the far bridge (`isle-swings.js`).
- **Quality tiers:** the aurora reflection is static on Balanced and Fast.
- **Languages:** native speakers should read the French, Spanish, Chinese and Hindi tables and listen to Mira's takes (`game/audio/voice/<id>/`); the Chinese takes use her English voice and two of them likely mispronounce (`camp-1`, `vault-done-frost` in `receipts/verification-languages/voices/zh/check-zh.json`), so regenerate them with a native Mandarin voice (`voices/scripts/native.mjs`) once Atlas generation answers again (it returned ACCESS_EXPIRED, HTTP 402) (`game/i18n/`, glossaries in `receipts/verification-languages/glossaries/`); Chinese and Hindi need a look on Windows, Android and Safari fonts; lines that count seconds still say "1 seconds" in English; Arabic would need a right-to-left layout.
- **Nightly schedule:** see [nightly review](nightly-review.md); the owner decides whether to install the LaunchAgent or the cron line.
