# Proposed next epics

Larger directions for the coming days, for the owner to choose and order. The previous list's epics are built (voice and scenes, the builders' vault, a verb per world, growing combat, light, sound, the loading engine, accessibility and the nightly review); these follow from them. Each keeps the original character, worlds and `far-door-` saves. Sizes are rough working days with verification.

## Story

1. **Mira's map:** the one epic not yet chosen. A pause screen and, after the ending, the court gate as a hub: choose any reached world's address on the medallions, see each world's relics, optional trails and drills, and return to finish them. About 3 days.
2. **Beyond the next door:** the vault ends on a door to an unknown world, today a scene of boxes. Build it as an eighth world with its own identity and one new verb, where Nyra and Mira cross together for the first time, so the sequel hook becomes a level. About 6 days.
3. **Mira in person:** she is a voice and a light. Give her a body through the same anatomical workflow as Nyra (head, garments, rig), seen in the camp memories and at a reunion where her light takes form, with lines timed to her mouth. About 6 days.

## Play

4. **A creature for every world:** only the frozen reach gained one (the lynx). Design a signature animal for the court and the checkpoint's night (the isles stay free of creatures by the style lock), each with a readable windup, an orb answer and a pistol answer, and a small bestiary in the relic reader. About 5 days.
5. **Puzzles for the new abilities:** the orb's pull finds only creatures today and the shield mostly the vault. Give earlier worlds optional trails that ask for both (pulling a loose block or a lantern across a gap, shielding through a spore field), plus a challenge mode that times the routes. About 4 days.

## Craft

6. **Sculpted builds:** the frozen falls, the castle's outer wall and bastion and the swing stakes are built in code with flat materials; take them through the recipe's candidates and verifier, with the climbs' contacts checked again. About 3 days.
7. **Listening, devices and players:** a physical phone, a gamepad, slower graphics and Safari; the whole mix, Mira's voice and Nyra's efforts on headphones; and the first ten minutes watched with fresh players, since the scripted naive player only approximates them. About 3 days.
